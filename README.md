## Video Creation & Editing

- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/video-editor) - Video editing and design for creators.
- [Adobe Premiere Pro](https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere.html) - Professional video editing suite.
- [Final Cut Pro](https://www.apple.com/final-cut-pro) - Mac-based professional video editor.
- [Runway](https://runway.com) - AI-powered video editing and effects.
- [Descript](https://www.descript.com) - Audio & video editor with AI transcription.

## Audio & Music

- [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org) - Free audio recording & editing software.
- [Epidemic Sound](https://www.epidemicsound.com) - Royalty-free music for creators.
- [Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com) - Subscription-based royalty-free music.
- [Anchor](https://creators.spotify.com) - Podcast creation & hosting platform.
- [Auphonic](https://auphonic.com) - Automatic audio leveling & enhancement.

## Graphics & Design

- [Canva](https://www.canva.com) - Templates, social media graphics, thumbnails.
- [Figma](https://www.figma.com) - Collaborative design for UI/UX and graphics.
- [Envato Elements](https://elements.envato.com) - Stock photos, templates, and creative assets.
- [Adobe Creative Cloud](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud.html) - Photoshop, Illustrator, and more.
- [Placeit](https://placeit.net) - Mockups, logos, and social media templates.

## Content Planning & Social Media

- [Buffer](https://buffer.com) - Schedule and publish content across platforms.
- [Hootsuite](https://www.hootsuite.com) - Social media management & analytics.
- [Later](https://later.com) - Instagram and TikTok scheduling tool.
- [Trello](https://trello.com) - Plan content calendars and workflows.
- [Airtable](https://www.airtable.com) - Database & content planning for teams.

## Monetization & Membership

- [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com) - Membership platform for recurring income.
- [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com) - Sell digital products & memberships.
- [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com) - Donations and memberships for creators.
- [Substack](https://substack.com) - Paid newsletters and subscriptions.
- [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com) - Simple support/donation platform.

## Analytics & Optimization

- [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com) - YouTube optimization & growth tools.
- [VidIQ](https://vidiq.com) - YouTube SEO & analytics.
- [Google Analytics](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics) - Track audience behavior and traffic.
- [Hotjar](https://www.hotjar.com) - Heatmaps & user behavior insights.
- [TKMetrics TikTok Money Calculator](https://tkmetrics.com/tiktok-money-calculator/) - Estimates monthly TikTok Creator Rewards and a sponsored-video rate from a username or your own numbers.
